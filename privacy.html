Overview

Bet&Win ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected when you use the app and how it is used.

Bet&Win does not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information directly.

Data We Collect

Bet&Win itself does not collect or store any personal data on our servers. All betting history and preferences you enter in the app are stored locally on your device only.

However, the app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect the following data to serve relevant ads:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device type, OS version, and language
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy.

📄 Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising

Ads displayed in Bet&Win are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show you personalised ads based on your interests.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID"

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties, except as described above in relation to Google AdMob's advertising services.

Data Retention

Since Bet&Win stores all user data locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app. We have no access to this data and cannot retrieve it.

Children's Privacy

Bet&Win is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has used the app, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
